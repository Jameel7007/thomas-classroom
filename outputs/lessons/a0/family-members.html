Family members

Name the people closest to you. Learn the family words in female / male pairs — mother / father, sister / brother — and join them with my, his, her and 's: my sister, Omar's brother, Ana's husband.

We say one thing many ways: mother = mom, father = dad, grandmother = grandma, grandfather = grandpa, children = kids. Both are correct and adult; mom / dad / grandma / grandpa / kids are warmer and more informal.

Notice before the rule

Read about Sofia's family

"My mother is a nurse. My father is a teacher. I have one sister and one brother. My sister's husband, Diego, is my favorite cousin's friend."

  • Which words name women in the family? Which name men? Can you find the pairs?
  • What do you think parents means — only the mother, only the father, or both together?
  • Look at my sister's husband. The little 's shows the sister has a husband. Who does Diego belong to?

Meaning check

  • How do we say more than one child? Not childs. The plural of child is children (warmly, kids).
  • What is the difference between parents and grandparents? Parents = your mother and father. Grandparents = your parents' parents.
  • Is cousin male or female? Either. One word for both: my cousin Ana, my cousin Tom.

Whose person is it?  my · his · her · 's

Put a possessive in front to show whose the person is. Keep these chunks together:

my mother   ·   her brother   ·   his wife

For a name, add 's to the person who "has" the family member:

Omar's brother   ·   Ana's husband   ·   my sister's son

Use it in real life

Your close family: Name four people in your family (real or invented) with a full sentence each. This is my mother, Carla. This is my brother, Luis.
Who do you live with? Say who is in your home now. I live with my wife and our two children.  I live with my parents.
A coworker's family: Describe a coworker's family using his / her and 's. My coworker Omar has a wife, Leyla. Omar's daughter is six.
A family photo: Show or imagine a photo. Point and name each person. This is my grandmother. These are my cousins.
Ask and answer: The teacher asks; the student answers in full. Do you have brothers or sisters? How many cousins do you have? Who is the oldest in your family?

Reflect

Say the pairs back

Complete this aloud: “A female parent is a ______; a male parent is a ______. Together they are my ______. My parents' parents are my ______. The plural of child is ______.”

Then say two true sentences about your own family using my and one sentence about a friend's family using 's.
