Vocabulary · Lesson 14

Classroom objects

Name the things on your desk and screen. Use a or an for one — a pen, an eraser — and add -s for more — two pens. Then follow simple instructions: Open your book. Look at the screen.

Most words add -s for "many": pen → pens, book → books, desk → desks. After a number, the noun is plural: two pens, three books.

The system: name the object, count the object

Here are the things around a study setup. Each one is a noun:

a book · a notebook · a pen · a pencil · an eraser · paper · a desk · a chair · a board · a bag · a laptop · a phone · a marker · a ruler · a screen

1. One thing?  Use a before a consonant sound, an before a vowel sound: a pen, an eraser.

2. More than one?  Drop a / an and add -s: two pens, three books, five chairs.

Meaning check

  • Can we say a eraser? No. Eraser starts with a vowel sound, so we say an eraser.
  • Can we say two book? No. After a number we need the plural: two books.
  • Is paper different? Yes. We usually say paper or some paper, not a paper, for the material you write on.

a / an in real instructions

In a lesson you will hear short instructions. Keep these chunks together:

Open your book.   ·   Write your name.   ·   Listen and repeat.
Look at the screen.   ·   Close the door.

And small location chunks: It's on the desk.   ·   in the bag.   ·   under the chair.

Use it in real life

Name your desk: Look at your real desk now. Name five objects with a or an: a pen, a notebook, an eraser, a phone, a laptop.
Count them: Now count. Say how many of each you have. I have two pens. I have one notebook. I have three books.
Where is it?: Hold up an object and say where it is. It's on the desk. It's in my bag. It's under the chair.
Follow the teacher: Listen and do it. Open your book. Write your name. Look at the screen. Close the door. Then give the teacher an instruction.
Information gap: The teacher names an object and you point to it on your desk or screen — or you describe one and the teacher guesses: You write with it. It's on the desk.
