Animals

Name common animals and choose a or an — a dog, an elephant. Then talk about animals in your life: I have a cat. It is small.

Notice: a or an?

Listen to the first sound

a cat  ·  a dog  ·  a fish
an elephant  ·  an owl  ·  an ant

  • What small word comes before cat, dog, fish?
  • What small word comes before elephant, owl, ant?
  • Listen to the first sound. When do we say an instead of a?

Drill 1 — choose a or an

Say the animal aloud first. Listen to its first sound.

1 cat  (first sound: c)
2 elephant  (first sound: e)
3 dog  (first sound: d)
4 owl  (first sound: o)
5 fish  (first sound: f)
6 ant  (first sound: a)

Build the meaning, then the sentence

Drill 2 — match each animal to a true description

Tap an animal, then tap the description that fits it

Drill 3 — build the sentence

Tap the words in order to make a correct sentence

Quick check

Read the clue, then choose the animal

1. It is very big and gray, with big ears. It is ___.

2. It lives in water and can swim. It is ___.

3. Many people keep it at home as a pet. It is ___.

4. It is small and can fly. It is ___.

Use it in real life

Talk about you: Do you have a pet? Say I have a cat. It is small and gray. If not: I don't have a pet, but I like dogs.
Pair task: name three animals you can see near your home or city, with a or an: a bird, a dog, a cat.

Reflect

Quick self-check

  • Can you name five animals without looking?
  • Do you say an before elephant, owl, ant — and a before the others?
  • Can you say one true sentence: I have a… or I like…?
Next use: in your next class, describe one animal in three short sentences: what it is, its size, and its color.
