Grammar · Lesson 01

The verb to be

The most common verb in English. We use to be to talk about who we are, how we feel, our age, and where things are.

≈ 25 min am / is / are Present simple
NoticeSee that be connects two ideas.
DiscoverChoose am, is, or are from the subject.
DrillBuild statements, negatives, and questions.
UseTalk about yourself and real people.

Notice first

Think it through

I am Ana.   My name is Ana.   We are students.

  • Which word changes after I, name, and we?
  • Are these sentences actions, or are they descriptions?
  • What is the word be connecting?

The core idea

Most verbs describe an action — run, eat, study. The verb to be is different. It describes a state: identity, feeling, age, job, nationality, or location.

Think of be as an equals sign for language:

I am Ana. means I = Ana.   She is happy. means she = happy.   They are at work. means they = at work.

Teaching move

  • Ask the student to point: I points to self, you points to the listener, they points to two people.
  • Do not start with a grammar table. Let the student feel the pattern first.
  • Then name the forms: I am, he is, we are.

Drill 2 — match the subject

Tap a subject tile, then tap the matching be form.

Negatives and questions

Negative: add not after be: I am not, she is not, they are not.

Question: move be before the subject: Are you a student? Is she from Spain? Am I late?

The important Thinking Method point: with be, English already has a helper. Do not add do.

Watch out — common beginner mistakes

  • ✗ I have 20 years.  →  I am 20 (years old).
  • ✗ She happy.  →  She is happy. (do not drop be)
  • ✗ Do you are a student?  →  Are you a student?

Drill 3 — build the question

Tap the words in order. Then ask the question aloud.

Drill 4 — spot the error

Tap the word that makes this question wrong.

Drill 5 — transform aloud

Student says the answer first. Then reveal and compare.

1 You are happy. → question

Are you happy?

2 She is at home. → negative

She is not at home.

3 They are friends. → question

Are they friends?

Use it

Personal truth: Say five true sentences: I am..., I am not..., My name is..., I am from..., I am a...
Mini interview: Ask and answer: Are you from Mexico? Are you a teacher? Are you tired today? Use short answers: Yes, I am. / No, I am not.

Final quiz — can you use be?

1. My name ___ Ana.

2. ___ you from Brazil?

3. She is ___ at home.

4. Yes, I ___.

5. They ___ my friends.
