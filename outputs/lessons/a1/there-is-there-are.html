There’s is the usual spoken form of there is. Use there are for plural nouns.

Get to the root: introduce something into the scene

There is / there are answers the basic question “What exists or is available here?” It is an introducing structure. The listener does not need to know about the thing before you mention it.

Existence first, location second

There is a café on this street. introduces a café into the conversation. The café is on this street. identifies the location of a café that both people already know about.

  • There’s a problem. = I am introducing the problem.
  • The problem is in this file. = We already know which problem; now I locate it.

“There” has a grammar job

In this pattern, there does not usually mean “in that place.” It holds the subject position so English can introduce new information after the verb. Compare There is a bank there: the first there builds the structure; the second one really means “in that place.”

The noun controls is or are

Look forward to the first noun after the verb. One countable thing or an uncountable substance takes is; plural things take are.

noun ideaformexample
one thingthere isThere is a message.
uncountable amountthere isThere is some water.
more than onethere areThere are three messages.

In quick informal speech, you may hear There’s two people outside. Careful standard English uses There are two people outside, so that is the form to build first.

Questions, negatives, and useful answers

Because is/are is the working verb, it moves before there in a question. It also carries not in a negative. No do/does is needed.

questionshort answernatural extra information
Is there a restroom?Yes, there is.It’s near the entrance.
Are there any seats?No, there aren’t.But there are some outside.

Articles and quantity still matter

Use a/an with one countable noun: There is a pharmacy. Use a number or some with plural nouns. In ordinary questions and negatives, any is common: Are there any pharmacies? There aren’t any nearby.
