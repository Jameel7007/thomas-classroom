5. Teacher evidence

Speaking task · 4 points

First-meeting role play: Have a short conversation with the student. Do not let them read complete prepared sentences.

Award one point for each ability shown independently or with one light prompt.

Writing task · 2 points

Give the student a blank mini form with Name, Nationality, Date of birth, then ask for a two-sentence personal introduction.

Award one point for each ability shown without copying a model.

Complete the sections, then check the assessment.
