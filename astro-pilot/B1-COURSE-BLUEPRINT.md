# B1 course blueprint

The B1 path moves the learner from describing familiar experiences to sustaining
connected explanations, recommendations, and problem-solving conversations. Each
lesson follows the release criteria in `CONTENT-QUALITY-STANDARD.md`.

| Seq. | Lesson | Independent-use outcome |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 1 | Present perfect continuous | Connect duration or recent activity to evidence now. |
| 2 | Past perfect | Make earlier causes and event order clear in past narratives. |
| 3 | Second conditional | Explore imagined choices and give tactful advice. |
| 4 | Used to / would | Contrast past states and routines with life now. |
| 5 | Passive voice | Shift focus to processes, results, and affected things. |
| 6 | Reported speech | Relay statements with accurate viewpoint and time reference. |
| 7 | Defining relative clauses | Identify people, things, places, and possessions precisely. |
| 8 | Modals of deduction | Calibrate present conclusions from evidence. |
| 9 | Question tags | Check information and invite agreement naturally. |
| 10 | Gerunds vs infinitives | Control common verb patterns and meaning-changing choices. |
| 11 | Future review | Distinguish immediate decisions, intentions, arrangements, and predictions. |
| 12 | Work and careers | Discuss roles, responsibilities, applications, and career development. |
| 13 | Environment and nature | Explain a problem as cause, effect, and practical response. |
| 14 | Media and news | Separate claims, sources, evidence, uncertainty, and opinion. |
| 15 | Relationships | Describe connection and conflict and negotiate constructive boundaries. |
| 16 | Money and spending | Compare affordability, total cost, and long-term value. |
| 17 | Crime and society | Report incidents neutrally and discuss evidence, process, and prevention. |
| 18 | Collocations and word families | Retrieve natural word partnerships and flexible word forms. |
| 19 | Discourse linkers | Build a coherent recommendation with reasons, contrast, examples, and conclusion. |

## Level transfer task

The end-of-level diagnostic asks the learner to understand a connected proposal,
evaluate evidence and practical trade-offs, present and defend a recommendation,
and write a 130–170 word argument. B1 is released only when all 19 lessons are
`ready` and the automatic diagnostic, route, asset, audio, and interaction gates pass.
