Daniel left the office at 9:15 on Friday night. He had planned to leave at seven, but a coworker needed help with a report. By the time he reached Central Station, most of the shops were closing and only a few people were waiting on the <ReadingWord term="platform" slug="platform" meaning="The raised area beside a train or bus where passengers wait." chunks={["wait on the platform", "Platform 4"]} />.

He was checking the departure board when an <ReadingWord term="announcement" slug="announcement" meaning="A public spoken or written message that gives information." chunks={["make an announcement", "station announcement"]} /> began. His train was canceled because a tree had fallen across the track. The next train would leave at 5:40 the following morning.

Daniel opened a travel app. The final bus on another <ReadingWord term="route" slug="route" meaning="The path followed by a bus, train, or traveler." chunks={["bus route", "take a different route"]} /> had left six minutes earlier. He was <ReadingWord term="stranded" slug="stranded" meaning="Unable to leave a place because transportation or help is unavailable." chunks={["stranded at the station", "leave someone stranded"]} /> at the station until morning. A taxi home would cost almost as much as he had earned that evening. He called his sister, but she was sleeping and did not answer.

As Daniel was standing outside the station, rain began to fall. A small blue car stopped beside him. The driver lowered the window.

“You live near Park Street, don’t you?” she asked.

Daniel did not know her name, but he <ReadingWord term="recognized" slug="recognize" meaning="Knew someone or something because he had seen it before." chunks={["recognize a face", "immediately recognize"]} /> her face. She worked at the bakery near his apartment. A paper bag with the bakery’s orange logo was sitting on the back seat.

“I’m going that way,” she said. “I can take you if you want.”

Daniel looked at the rain, then at the familiar logo. He sent his sister the car’s license number and accepted the ride.

While they were driving, rain was hitting the windows and the streets were almost empty. The woman introduced herself as Rosa. She explained that she was returning from her own late <ReadingWord term="shift" slug="shift" meaning="A scheduled period when someone works." chunks={["late shift", "finish a shift"]} />. They talked about the bakery, rising rent in the neighborhood, and the best place to buy coffee before seven.

Rosa dropped Daniel outside his building twenty minutes later. He thanked her and promised to visit the bakery on Saturday.

Daniel had always believed that asking for help made a person look unprepared. That night, he understood something different. Sometimes the safest route home was not the one he had planned.
