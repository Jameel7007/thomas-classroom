## What to observe in every pilot

1. Can the learner explain why `she works` changes but `does she work` does not?
2. Which controlled item produces a wrong answer for a reason other than the target grammar?
3. Does the first hint help the learner self-correct without revealing the answer?
4. Can the learner complete at least three personalized sentences and two questions without copying?
5. Does the 45–55 minute core path preserve enough time for final production?
6. On the next day, can the learner complete the five-minute retrieval without rereading the rule?

## Promotion rule

After each completed pilot, increment `qualityReview.learnerPilotCount` in the
lesson metadata. Change the status to `learner-pilot` after the first completed
session. Mark the lesson `reviewed` only after all three records are complete,
the answer and timing concerns have been resolved, and a named tutor signs off.
At that point, remove `tutorReviewRequired` (or set it to `false`) and add
`reviewedBy` plus `reviewedOn` in `YYYY-MM-DD` format. The schema rejects a
`reviewed` claim with fewer than three pilots or without that sign-off.
